Welcome to Our Food Blog

Welcome to our food blog, a place dedicated to the rich and flavorful traditions of Ethiopian and Eritrean cuisine.

Here we share stories, recipes, and cultural insights behind some of our favorite dishes. From the comforting taste of injera to the bold spices found in stews like doro wat and zigni, this blog celebrates food that brings people together.

Our goal is to preserve tradition, explore flavors, and introduce more people to the beauty of Ethiopian and Eritrean food. Whether you grew up with these dishes or are discovering them for the first time, we invite you to enjoy the journey with us.
